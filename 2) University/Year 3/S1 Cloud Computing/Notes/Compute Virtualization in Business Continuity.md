## Advantages of Compute Virtualization in BC
- Hardware independence
- Cross platform compatibility
- Mutual isolation
	- Each VM operates independently, meaning failures in one VM doesn't necessarily affect other VMs
- Encapsulation of complete computing environment
	- VM can package its OS, apps, and configs, allowing easy backup, copy, and restore
- Relatively robust BC processes
	- Virtualization simplifies disaster recovery through snapshots, replication, etc
