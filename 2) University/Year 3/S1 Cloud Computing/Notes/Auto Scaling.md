## Auto Scaling
The process of dynamically allocating resources as required by an application, to match performance requirements, whilst also minimizing runtime costs

It applies to all resources used by an application, not just computing resources.

## Types of Auto Scaling
Scaling can either be done:
- Vertically (scaling up) for more powerful instances
- horizontally (scaling out) or more instances
![[Pasted image 20261009124313.png]]


### Vertical Auto Scaling
Referred to as scaling up or scaling down
- Hardware is modiied, to expand or reduce its capacity and performance
- Hardware is redeployed with alternative hardware, with the appropriate capacity and performance
- Is a disruptive process, requiring the system to be unavailable whilst its being redeployed. (NOT COMMON)

### Horizontal Auto Scaling
Referred to as scaling out or scaling in
