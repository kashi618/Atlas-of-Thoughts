## BC - Business Continuity
Refers to an organisation's ability to keep critical business operations running during and after disruptions. These include:
- Cyberattacks
- Power outtages
- Hardware failures
- Natural disasters
- Cloud service failures

It focuses on keeping the entire business functioning, even during disruptions

## DR - Disaster Recovery
Focuses specifically on how IT systems can restore apps and data after a disruption
