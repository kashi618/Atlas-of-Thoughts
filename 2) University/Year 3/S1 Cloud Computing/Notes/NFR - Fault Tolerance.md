## Fault Tolerance in Distributed Systems
In any computer system hardware, software, and networks can and will fail. A fault tolerant system must maintain availablity to its services, even at low levels of hardware/software/network reliability.

It is commonly achieved using:
- Redundancy
  Extra components are added, such that if one fails, the extra component can take over the workload
- Replication
  Copying and creating active synced backups of data and services across various servers and regions. When data is written, it is written to multiple servers at once
