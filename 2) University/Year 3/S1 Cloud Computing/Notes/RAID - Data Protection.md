## What is, and Why RAID?
**Redundant array of independent disks**

It is a technique that combines multiple disk drives into a logical unit called a RAID set. This unit provides protection, performance, or both.
- Due to the physical/mechanical nature of disk drives, it often limits performance
- Each individual drive also has a certain life expectancy, and can be measured using the MTBF
- RAID was introduced to mitigate these problems

### MBTF - Mean Time Between Failures
For example, if a drive has an average file time of 750,000 hours, and there are 1,000 drives in an array, the MTBF is 750 hours.
$$\frac{750,000}{1,000}=750\text{ Hours}$$
