In the case of a virtual data centres, solutions to implement business continuity needs to protect both the physical and virtualized resources. This includes resources at all layers, such as:
- Compute
- Storage
- Network
- Etc

To enable BC in a VDC, requires consistent copies of data and redundant infrastructure components
