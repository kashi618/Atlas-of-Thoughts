## What is Artificial AI?


**AI**
The field of study

**ML**
The branch of AI, focusing on intelligent machines that learn from data. Has the same output every time.

**Deep Learning**
A subset of machine learning, based on artificial neural networks. Has the same output every time.
Examples: CNNs, RNNs

**Generative AI**
A type of ANN that actually generates data, similar to the data it was trained on. May not have the same output everytime.

Examples: GANs, LLMs

## Goals of AI
To implement human intelligence in machines. Creating systems that understand, think, learn, and behave like humans

### Examples of AI

- Smart assistants
- E-payments
- Search algorithms
- Media streaming
- Smart cars
- navigation apps

- Text editors
