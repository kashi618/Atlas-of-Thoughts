## Intelligent Systems
**Computer-based systems that percieve, reason, learn, and act intelligently in their environment to achieve specific goals**

### Key Characteristics
- Adaptive and responsive
- Operate autonomously (without interaction by humans)
- Decision-making capabilities

Examples:
- Virtual assistants (Alexa, siri, etc)
- Autonomous robots

### Types of Intelligent Systems
**Expert Systems**
- Specialized in a domain to solve problems
- Example: MYCIN for medical diagnosis

**Interactive Systems**
- Engage users
- Example: Voice assistants

**Autonomous Systems**
- Acts independently in real time
- Example: Drones, robotic vacuum cleaners

**Hybrid Systems**
- Combines multiple capabilities
- Example: smart assistants in IoT

### Components
**Sensors and Perception**
Collects data from the environment. Such as cameras, microphones, GPS services.
Example: self-driving car use LIDAR and cameras to percieve its surroundings

**Knowledge Representation**
Organizes and stores data for reasoning.
Example: semantic networks, ontologies

**Decision-Making and Reasoning**
Derive conclusions and make choices
Example: AI in healthcare, helping diagnose diseases

**Learning Capabilities**
Using machine learning to improve performance overtime
Example: Chatbots learning from user queries, to get better/more personalized responses
