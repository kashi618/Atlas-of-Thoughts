## Spudman - Microprocessor Project
During my embedded sytstems module at TUD, I worked in a team of three to develop an interactive game on the STM32 microcontroller. This was essentially a small CPU connected to a circuit board, allowing you to write code to it, attach buttons, and displays

When we where working on thips chip,t he biggest problem we ran into was the limited amount of memory. The model we used, only held 32KB of flash memory. To put this into perspective, a normal picture you take on your phone is around 3MB which is almost 100 times bigger than the storage.

As we started to code the game and graphics, we quickly hit the limit on our storage, as we have a lot of functionality we wanted to add. So I took initiative and asked my two teammates to continue working on coding the game, whilst I researched on ways to reduce our memory usage.

As a result, I found that our graphics and ui took up the most amount of storage. Each graphic was stored as an image, so instead I converted them all into vector graphics instead. What this means is that, in our case, we needed an image of grass, and instead of uploading a picture of it, I could use a mathematical formulas instead to calculate and draw out triangles, which would mimic what grass looked like

In the end, I was able to reduce all the graphics enough, where we were able to compelete our game without running out of memory using just over 95% of the 32KB. 

I am very proud of this project, as it shows my ability to take up a difficult problem, research for solutions, but also to push this chip to its limit
