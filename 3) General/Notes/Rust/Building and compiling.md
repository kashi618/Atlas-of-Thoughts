## Rustc
- `rustc` - simply compiles files, such as gcc

## Cargo
- `cargo build` - compiles files, and stores them in `target/debug/main`
- `cargo build --release` - compiles files with optimizations, and stores them in `target/release/main`
- `cargo run` - compiles files, and runs the executable
- `cargo check` - sees if compilation is sucessfull (does not build files, meaning its faster if you just want to check)
